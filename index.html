NOC-2425 Incident Operations 24/7 Coverage 2024–2025 · US ENTERPRISE & RETAIL NETWORKS

Two years inside a US SD-WAN NOC: five platforms, hundreds of sites

At Zayo I monitored and ran incident response for US enterprise and retail networks across VeloCloud, Meraki, Prisma, Versa and Cradlepoint, with ServiceNow for tickets and BigPanda for alert correlation. Three incidents that show what the job actually is:

Carrier circuit died overnight. Nobody noticed.

A site's primary transport dropped mid-shift. SD-WAN steered traffic to the secondary ISP and the quality score held at 10/10 for voice, video and transactional traffic while the carrier ticket ran. The dashboard shows the dead link and the untouched user experience side by side.

Cloud security tunnels down at a branch

Both Zscaler cloud security tunnels from a site's edge dropped. Isolated the failure to the provider side rather than the edge device, escalated with evidence attached, and kept traffic on the backup path until the tunnels restored.

Store phones offline at a retail site

A retail location's VoIP gateway reported both lines down in the monitoring portal. Remote diagnosis and reprovisioning brought the site back without dispatching a technician, which is the difference between minutes and days for a store.

INC-3CX Incident Resolved CLIENT OFFICE · ABU DHABI

Phones down: isolate before you replace

Situation
A desk phone at a client office went dead. To the office it was "the network is broken again"; the 3CX endpoint showed up on screen but the line would not register.
Action
Swap-tested along the chain: known-good handset on the same cable, wall port and switch port all came straight online. Fault isolated to the handset's own LAN port, not the network.
Result
One handset replaced and reprovisioned on the phone system. No time wasted rebuilding a network that was never broken, which is the entire discipline.

Method

How I work

Four habits from carrier operations that come with me to every environment, whatever the size.

01 · DIAGNOSE FIRST

Isolate before you replace

Swap tests, evidence, elimination. The phone story above is the whole philosophy in one ticket: prove where the fault is before spending anyone's money or rebuilding anything.

02 · CHANGE DISCIPLINE

Windows, MOPs, rollback plans

100+ production changes with zero unplanned downtime did not happen by luck. Every planned work gets steps written down, a window agreed, and a way back if it goes sideways.

03 · PAPER TRAIL

Documented, serialed, signed

Part serials recorded, port maps printed, site reports filed within SLA, handovers photographed. Six months later, anyone can pick up where I left off, including me.

04 · NOC-GRADE COMMS

Status people can act on

Bridge-call cadence from carrier incident rooms: what happened, what's impacted, what's next, when you'll hear from me again. In plain English for whoever is listening.
